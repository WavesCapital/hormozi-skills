        # Hormozi Closing Framework

        ## Job
        Improve objection handling, call structure, and closer messaging.

        ## Use Cases
        - Our prospects keep objecting on price
- Give me closes for indecisive leads
- Fix this sales call script

        ## Outputs
        - objection-handling matrix
- close tree/script
- closer drill sheet
- call review rubric

        ## Hard Boundaries
        - Not pre-call nurture
- Not offer redesign
- Not retention/customer-success operations

        ## Composition
        - Composes with: hormozi-offers, hormozi-lead-nurture, hormozi-launch-campaigns
        - Preferred sequence: Use after the offer is solid and before optimizing team training systems.
        - Fallbacks: none
