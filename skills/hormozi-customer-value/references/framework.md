        # Hormozi Customer Value Framework

        ## Job
        Increase post-purchase customer value through retention, expansion, and fast-cash moves.

        ## Use Cases
        - How do we make each customer worth more?
- Churn is killing us
- We need cash in 30 days without new acquisition

        ## Outputs
        - LTV/Crazy-Eight diagnosis
- churn audit
- expansion ladder
- fast-cash campaign sequence

        ## Hard Boundaries
        - Not acquisition strategy
- Not standalone price architecture
- Not full business model redesign

        ## Composition
        - Composes with: hormozi-pricing, hormozi-money-models, hormozi-proof
        - Preferred sequence: Use after acquisition and offer basics are working.
        - Fallbacks: none
