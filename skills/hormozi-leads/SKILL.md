# Hormozi Leads

Diagnose acquisition bottlenecks and recommend lead-generation channels, lead magnets, CTAs, and lead-getter systems.

## Use Cases

- We need more qualified leads
- Which acquisition channels should we use first?
- Why did our lead flow stall?

## Primary Outputs

- acquisition constraint audit
- prioritized lead-channel plan
- CTA/lead-magnet map
- lead-getter mix

## Hard Boundaries

- Not full ad production
- Not sales objection handling
- Not lead follow-up/show-rate systems

## Composition

- Composes with: `hormozi-hooks`, `hormozi-goated-ads`, `hormozi-lead-nurture`
- Preferred sequence: Use before hooks or ads when the core problem is lead volume or lead source quality.
