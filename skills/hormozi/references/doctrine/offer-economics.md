# Offer & Economics

Writing rule: `HZ-SYN-004`. Put exact money terms in plain words before you give advice or copy.

Improve the promise, value, packaging, price, risk reversal, monetization sequence, and unit economics as one system.

## Workflow

1. Verify the market, customer outcome, current offer, and delivery economics.
2. Apply the value equation and obstacle-to-solution process.
3. Model price, margin, payment timing, expansion, and continuity together.
4. Add honest enhancers only after the core value works.

## Material Rules

### HZ-OFE-001 — Value equation

Increase perceived value by increasing the dream outcome and perceived likelihood while decreasing perceived delay and perceived effort or sacrifice.

Decision logic:

- Name the customer's desired end state.
- List evidence that makes success believable.
- Shorten time to first and full value.
- Remove work, uncertainty, and sacrifice from the customer.

### HZ-OFE-002 — Perceived value must be communicated

Operational improvements create commercial value only when the prospect can perceive their effect on success, delay, effort, or outcome.

Decision logic:

- Improve the actual mechanism first.
- Make the improvement observable in proof, demonstration, packaging, or expectation setting.
- Do not substitute perception claims for delivery.

Citations:

- `source:100m-offers-alex-hormozi:p0082` (primary)

### HZ-OFE-003 — Differentiate into a category of one

A strong offer reduces direct price comparison by combining a distinctive mechanism, promotion, value proposition, premium price, risk reversal, and workable payment model.

Decision logic:

- Identify what buyers currently compare.
- Create a meaningful difference tied to the desired outcome.
- Support the difference with proof and delivery, not naming alone.

Citations:

- `source:100m-offers-alex-hormozi:p0041` (primary)
- `source:100m-offers-alex-hormozi:p0042` (primary)

### HZ-OFE-004 — Turn obstacles into solutions

Build the offer by enumerating every obstacle between the customer and the dream outcome, then translating each obstacle into a solution component.

Decision logic:

- List the required customer steps.
- Use the four value drivers to expose reasons each step may fail.
- Reverse each obstacle into a solution statement.

Citations:

- `source:100m-offers-alex-hormozi:p0107` (primary)
- `source:100m-offers-alex-hormozi:p0111` (visual_example)

### HZ-OFE-005 — Choose delivery vehicles, then trim and stack

Generate many delivery vehicles, remove low-value components, and retain high-value elements whose delivery economics remain viable.

Decision logic:

- Diverge before judging delivery options.
- Remove high-cost low-value, then low-cost low-value items.
- Retain low-cost high-value and justified high-cost high-value items.
- Step back toward a scalable version without destroying the value driver.

Citations:

- `source:100m-offers-alex-hormozi:p0116` (primary)
- `source:100m-offers-alex-hormozi:p0121` (primary)

### HZ-OFE-006 — Enhancers amplify a real offer

Use genuine scarcity, truthful urgency, valuable bonuses, risk-reversing guarantees, and clear names to increase demand after the core value is sound.

Decision logic:

- Validate the core outcome and delivery first.
- Tie every enhancer to a real operational condition.
- Remove fabricated deadlines, false scarcity, and unsupported guarantees.

Limits:

- Legal, regulatory, platform, and claims constraints override persuasion tactics.

Citations:

- `source:100m-offers-alex-hormozi:p0140` (primary)

### HZ-OFE-007 — Reverse the specific risk

Design a guarantee around the prospect's actual feared failure and conditions the business can fairly verify and fulfill.

Decision logic:

- Name the risk blocking purchase.
- Choose a conditional, unconditional, anti-guarantee, or implied structure appropriate to delivery.
- Specify customer responsibilities and the remedy without hiding material terms.

Limits:

- Do not guarantee lender approvals, financing outcomes, health, legal, financial, or other outcomes outside the business's control.

Citations:

- `source:100m-offers-alex-hormozi:p0169` (primary)

### HZ-OFE-008 — Build a sequenced money model

Combine attraction, upsell, downsell, and continuity offers so acquisition becomes profitable quickly and customer value grows over time.

Decision logic:

- Use attraction to convert strangers.
- Offer the next solution at the moment of greatest need.
- Use payment or feature downsells instead of treating every no as final.
- Add continuity only where ongoing value is real.

Citations:

- `source:100m-money-models-how-to-make-money:p0023` (primary)
- `source:100m-money-models-how-to-make-money:p0147` (primary)
- `source:100m-money-models-how-to-make-money:p0153` (primary)

### HZ-OFE-009 — Optimize price for total gross profit

Choose price from conversion, gross margin, retention, and lifetime gross profit together, not from close rate or churn alone.

Decision logic:

- Model each candidate price across conversion, units sold, churn, delivery cost, and lifetime gross profit.
- Test a bounded range.
- Select the price that maximizes durable gross profit within delivery and claims constraints.
