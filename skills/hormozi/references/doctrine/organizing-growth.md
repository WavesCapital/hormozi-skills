# When Growth Makes Work Messy

Writing rule: `HZ-SYN-004`. Keep the advice clear and easy to use.

Use this when a big firm loses track of work or control of costs. First find which loss matters most. Fix that part of the work before adding a new layer of bosses. The source calls this stage Categorize: sort work into clear groups.

## Give Each Fix An Owner

If product fixes take too long, find where they wait. Give a focused team one clear problem to solve. Keep its work tied to the buyer's result. A team for each feature can create more handoffs and delay.

Group work so the right person can act on it. For leads, that may mean fit and interest. For buyers, it may mean who needs help now. For hiring, it may mean the role and next step. Each group needs a reason, not just a name in a tool.

## Protect The Way You Get Buyers

One strong lead source can still be a risk. Ask what happens if it stops. Check the share of buyers and cash it brings. Then compare the harm with the cost to build a second path.

Keep the first path working while you test the next one. Choose from the buyers you can reach, your skills and your cash. A referral plan may fit; it is not the only choice. The source also allows a similar platform or a new lead method. Check whether the new path still has the same weak point as the first.

Set an owner and a test cost the firm can bear. Judge buyer fit and sales, not just cheap names. Use the real sales cycle to set the review date. Do not invent a fixed month or launch many paths at once to look bold.

The early-stage advice to focus and this advice to spread risk serve different needs. Choose from the firm's facts. A working first path does not have to fail before you protect it.

## Stop Losing Leads And Buyers

Give new interest a clear owner and enough staff cover to respond. Use a short follow-up plan so leads do not fall through gaps. Read `HZ-SAL-003` in [sales.md](sales.md) for when to call and when to hold the meeting.

Praise good show rates without making them the whole goal. A show rate is the share of booked people who attend. Check fit and sales too. The source says to give kudos; it does not set a pay plan.

Find where buyers get stuck before they complain or leave. Give that step an owner. Help them get the value they bought before pitching more. Give buyers one clear path across both products. Make sure both teams agree on the facts.

## Put Data And Money In The Right Place

Give each kind of data a clear home and owner. Central does not mean open to all. Passwords and private staff forms need access set for the people who need them. A new cloud folder alone does not make data safe.

Use a hiring tracker when volume makes candidates hard to track. Find the delay before buying a tool or paying a recruiter. Neither can fix a decision that sits on the owner's desk.

Give teams budgets with clear spending limits. Set which larger costs need approval. Do not make the owner approve every small item. Review actual spending against the plan and check what changed. The guide suggests a quarterly budget; exact limits depend on the firm.

These are choices for real problems, not a shopping list triggered by staff count. Check whether work flows better and buyers get the result. A tidier chart is not the goal.

For deeper specialist work or the next major growth choice, read [mature-growth.md](mature-growth.md). It covers the Stage8 and Stage9 lessons. Size alone is not a reason to buy tools or make a deal.
