## Start With Use, Value and Pay

Use [startup guidance](startup-stage.md) for Stages 0–2. Keep real use, paid demand and cash received distinct. A partner-paid firm need not invent a buyer fee or redo a free trial. First-sale headlines do not erase later repeat-sale and delivery checks. The Stage 2 teaching uses 100 minutes, not 100 calls. Its name does not require paid ads. Service stories do not set a permanent all-hours promise. Named sales and service methods need their full sources; these tables do not define them.

## Train Help and Choose the Right Work

Use [small-team guidance](small-team-growth.md) for Stages 3–4. Train for a real work gap, keep suitable slow buyers distinct from poor-fit leads, and measure the path to cash. A setter and closer solve different capacity limits. Choose customers from results and costs, not just the largest sale or one industry. Read [customer fit](customer-fit-review.md) when deciding whether more qualification helps. The payroll block is mislabeled Customer Service in Stage 2 page 28 and Stage 3 page 7; use the actual content, not that heading.

## Add a Product or Improve the Current Work

Use [next-product guidance](next-product-growth.md) for Stages 5–6. A next offer fits a real buyer need and weak value per customer; it is not a reward for reaching ten staff. Protect current delivery and cash. When added products slow the firm down, find a cause, test a change, teach it and check results. Fewer refunds can hide fewer sales. Compare customer groups at the same age and keep both sales skill and service results in view.

The Stage 6 table names ad production, individual sales coaching, team cadence, customer groups, talent beyond the founder's network and detailed money records. These are distinct possible work gaps, not a required project list. The linked guide routes them to fuller teaching.

## Give Work Owners Without Creating Silos

Use [organizing guidance](organizing-growth.md) for Stage 7. A second lead path may protect a working first path from risk. That does not conflict with early focus or require six new channels. Keep follow-up covered, prevent repeat service problems and give each type of data and spending a suitable owner. Central storage does not mean open access. A hiring tracker cannot fix a decision left waiting on the founder.

## Specialize and Protect the Core

Use [mature-growth guidance](mature-growth.md) for Stages 8–9. Check buyer value before pruning features. Keep one buyer path across specialist teams. Compare salespeople on comparable leads. Match systems and senior hires to real work. Consider a major next growth source without damaging the product that funds it; a year-plus horizon is not a promised payback.

Preserve the source's own distinctions:

- Stage 8 table 3 says founder final check; page 8 narrows this to important hires. Stage 9's senior recruiting work is not founder interviews for every routine job.
- Enterprise software **may** be needed in the Stage 9 preview in Stage 8 pages 19/22. It is not an automatic Stage 8 purchase.
- Stage 8 page 26 repeats its own page 9 after the Stage 9 preview. Stage 9 page 11 repeats Stage 8 material from page 19. Placement does not turn either into a new stage rule.
- Blended ad return uses actual total revenue, not overlapping platform claims. It is not profit or proof of a channel's cause. Optional funding and quality-of-earnings work are not an order to borrow or sell.

## Keep Context in Its Place

Company counts, headcounts and leadership layers describe the model; they do not establish this firm's limiting factor, its odds of success or current population statistics. Stage advice can fit one part of a firm without resetting every other part.

Skool and workshop offers are promotions, not required purchases. The one-in-two revenue claim, 35-day hiring claim and 90% hiring claim lack enough defined evidence here to become promises. Repeated ads and photos add no independent outcome proof.

Familiar comparisons in Stages 6–8 can make a hard idea easy. Follow the [shared writing rule](../communication-standard.md#write-copy); use a comparison only when it helps this reader. It explains a relationship, not proof of a result.

Training links and video stills point to separate media. Reading a PDF does not mean those videos were reviewed. Apply the task's video date/type limits before opening them. The short legal, tax, security and investment rows name needs; they do not supply full professional methods or authorize changes.
