## Review A Real Call Together

Use this when sales promises, ads and service do not line up. Leila calls it Gametape Review. Bring the people who own those parts of the buyer's path. Watch an actual call you have permission to use.

- Sales: What worked? What needs practice?
- Marketing: Which doubts can we clear up before the call? Which need sales training?
- Service: Which promises must reach our team? Which promises can we not keep?

Focus the review where work is failing. Choose one fix that can help most. Give it an owner and check the result in later calls. The source worksheet has room for three ideas, but its text says to act on one. Do not let the form set the workload.

Leila's setup uses a weekly 60–90 minute review led by sales. She gathers the call a day ahead and plays it at 1.5x speed. She suggests four weeks on a team with a known problem. Adapt the time and people to the need. This advice does not grant permission to record, share or schedule anything.

## Check The Whole Service Result

Leila names five parts of good service:

- **Concern:** Show that the buyer's problem matters. Check you understand it.
- **Courtesy:** Listen and treat the buyer well.
- **One and done:** Solve the whole issue when you can. Check what else it affects.
- **Educate and empower:** Give the answer first. Show how to avoid or solve the issue next time.
- **Timeliness:** Respond promptly. If the fix takes time, acknowledge the issue and explain the next step.

Kind words are not proof of a fix. A closed ticket is not proof either. Check what the buyer can now do and whether the issue comes back. Do not close work early to raise a score. Get help when needed. Keep an owner until the buyer's issue is fixed.

Keep customer scores separate from observed results. The supplied survey asks about all five parts, then asks for an overall score. That is six items, though the introduction says five. Use the extra overall score as a summary, not a sixth service skill.

Source: Same Leila PDF,6–11 for service behavior and12–13 for the survey. The reopened-issue check is our added way to test whether the fix holds.

## Make Team Talk Useful

Leila looks at three things:

- **Energy:** How much people interact and what that contact is like.
- **Engagement:** How widely people take part, not just how much the loudest person speaks.
- **Exploration:** How people share useful ideas across teams.

Find the weakest part. Ask the team what gets in the way. Invite quiet people to share in a way that works for them. Pick a change and check whether decisions or handoffs improve. More messages alone are not the goal.

The source's research claim about 40% of variation is not a promised 40% gain. Its diagram is a memory aid, not a formula for sales or output. It does not justify instant replies all day or judging staff by how loud they are. Set reply cover that fits the work and protects focused time.

## Plan The Week With Room To Work

Look back at work left open and why it slipped. Look ahead one to three months. Put named priority work on the calendar first. Then account for routine work, breaks and real commitments. Leave room for urgent requests and work that takes longer than planned.

A full calendar is not the aim. It should show what fits and what must move. Do not remove needed customer care to protect a project block. The colors and hours in the sample calendars are examples, not rules.

Source: Same Leila PDF,26–29 for the method and30–31 for calendar images. Its claim of 10–12 hours saved is not a guarantee for this team.

## Discuss Pay With Clear Reasons

Leila links pay growth to results, reliable work and added duties. A strong worker need not become a manager to earn more. Put a larger role in writing. Use current pay data for similar work and show the value the person brings.

Read32–37 when the user asks about her pay method. Her 2024 guide gives example raise bands, with overlap at 5% and 10%. Those bands are not current market rates or the user's pay policy. Its usual once-a-year timing has exceptions for promotion and special cases. Do not turn it into a ban on fair pay corrections.

Discuss the facts before naming a rate for a real firm. Check its current pay terms, budget and any legal duties. No source example grants the right to change pay. The raise request needs facts and a clear amount. It does not promise a yes.
