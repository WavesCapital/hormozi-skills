# Find the Next Step for a New Firm

Use this when a firm has an idea, early users or its first sales. Check what has happened. Do not pick a stage from revenue or team size alone.

## No One Has Used It Yet

The Stage 0 guide starts with no product or users. Its goal is real use of a free offer. Make a small thing that helps. It may be a service, a guide or a product. Start with people you can reach who need that help.

Help them use it. Ask what worked and what got in the way. Change it from what you learn. Keep costs low and tools simple. This small test does not need a large team or a full set of systems.

Praise is not the same as use. Look for use and a useful result. People who come back are a stronger sign than people who just like the idea.

Source: `source:your-acq-100mscalingroadmap-stage0:p0007` and `source:your-acq-100mscalingroadmap-stage0:p0009`.

## People Use It and Get Value

The next step is to test whether people will pay. The first paid version need not be perfect. It must be worth the price. Show what the paid offer gives them. Start with past users when they fit. Use their true results as proof.

Paying buyers may expect more. Check hidden costs like time and effort. Look for delays or trouble using the product. Fix what blocks the result, not every small flaw.

Check the whole pay path. A yes is not cash in the bank. Know who pays and what is due. Track the sale, the work and the cash received. Do not run a real charge without the user's approval.

Sources: `source:your-acq-100mscalingroadmap-stage0:p0015` and `source:your-acq-100mscalingroadmap-stage0:p0016`.

## One Sale Is a Start

The headline goal is a first sale. The later pages ask for sales to more than one buyer. They also call for useful results and basic systems that work. One favor from a friend does not prove you can scale ads.

Check the next missing proof. Do not reset a firm that already has it. Do not make all roadmap rows into gates for each small task.

Sources: `source:your-acq-100mscalingroadmap-stage0:p0019` and `source:your-acq-100mscalingroadmap-stage0:p0020`.

## Fit the Model

For a firm paid by a partner, the user and payer may differ. Trace who gets value and what earns the firm its pay. Do not invent a client fee or a new free product to fit the chart. This is our use of the roadmap, not a rule stated in it.

The guide's tool names are examples. Its Skool ad is not proof of likely profit. Its brief legal and tax tips are not full advice for every place or firm. Check those facts when they matter.

## When Sales Start and Stop

The next-stage preview in the Stage 1 guide adds a new need: steady customer flow. Keep a set time for outreach. Track both the work and the buyers it brings. Build on the path that works before adding more channels.

This guide uses 100 minutes a day as its Rule of 100. Minutes are not calls or sales. Check the full teaching for the chosen channel. Fit the plan to the firm's real time and costs. The stage name does not mean every firm must buy ads.

Fix early service gaps by hand when needed. Learn what breaks so the next buyer gets a better result. This is not a promise of free custom work forever or service at all hours.

Add help for a real skill or time gap. Do not hire just to match a chart. Keep roles and pay clear. Keep simple cash records and plan for bills and taxes.

The chart names sales and service methods but does not teach their steps. These include CLOSER and ACA. It also names Angry Boat and Epiphany Script. Open the full source before teaching a method. Do not guess from its name.
