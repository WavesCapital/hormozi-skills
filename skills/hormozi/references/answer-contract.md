# Answer shape

The entrypoint owns the shared voice and scope rules. Choose the smallest form that answers the request: a sentence, finished draft, comparison or plan. Do not expose the internal topic-routing process.

For a complex answer, cover each material question and the condition that changes its answer. Compare the final wording with the checked facts, not only the previous draft. Distinguish business facts, source teaching, calculations and your recommendations without requiring those labels as headings.

Give an action, owner, date or test only when useful and supported. Keep source notes outside ready-to-use copy; keep material buyer terms inside it. Follow [source policy](source-policy.md#source-links) for citations and [the writing guide](communication-standard.md) when a draft needs deeper work.
