# Mode Index

Writing rule: `HZ-SYN-004`. Do not copy hard internal labels into the answer. Use a plain name or define the exact term.

Scan every mode that could materially affect the result. For a narrow request, use one main mode and up to one support mode. For a complex decision or full business audit, consider every relevant mode and deeply inspect the two or three that drive the outcome. Always finish with one main constraint and a sequenced plan.

| Mode | Use when the constraint is | Read | Assets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diagnose | unclear stage, bottleneck, sequence, or priority | `doctrine/diagnose.md` | constraint diagnostic, stage scorecard, experiment card |
| Offer & economics | promise, packaging, price, risk reversal, monetization, or unit economics | `doctrine/offer-economics.md` | offer builder, economics model, money-model map |
| Demand | lead source, lead magnet, hook, ad, brand association, or a real launch | `doctrine/demand.md` | channel scorecard, creative test plan, launch brief |
| Sales | scheduling, show rate, proof, conversion, objections, or sales capacity | `doctrine/sales.md` | pipeline leakage audit, proof plan, call scorecard |
| Customer value | activation, retention, expansion, continuity, referrals, or fast cash | `doctrine/customer-value.md` | customer-value diagnostic, retention-expansion plan |
| Team & operations | verified people, handoff, service quality, communication, or cadence constraint | `doctrine/team-operations.md` | operating-cadence board, service-quality scorecard |

## Collision Rules

When deterministic scores tie, do not pick by wording alone. Validate business context, compare the downstream economic gain and evidence for each candidate constraint, and select the mode that owns the binding step. If current evidence cannot break the tie, ask one short question whose answer would.

- Low conversion plus repeated price objections: diagnose offer, proof, and lead quality before choosing closing.
- More leads requested with poor show or close throughput: sales may bind before demand.
- Pricing requested without delivery cost, conversion, or retention evidence: offer-economics first asks for the math.
- Brand requested for immediate revenue: demand separates long-horizon association from direct-response work.
- Team system requested at an early stage: diagnose whether the owner is importing overhead before the constraint requires it.
- Fast cash requested with neglected customers or broken delivery: customer-value protects trust before promotion.

## Compound Sequences

Modes connect. Targeting can change the offer, the offer can change sales, activation can create proof, and proof can improve demand. Use the table above to find those links without treating every mode as equally urgent.

- Diagnose → Offer & economics → Demand
- Diagnose → Sales → Offer & economics
- Diagnose → Customer value → Offer & economics
- Diagnose → Team & operations

The advisor owns the final synthesis and answer contract.
