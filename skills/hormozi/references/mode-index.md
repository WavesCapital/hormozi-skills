# Mode Index

Writing rule: `HZ-SYN-004`. Do not copy hard internal labels into the answer. Use a plain name or define the exact term.

Use these routes to find the right teaching, not to limit the answer. Start with the most relevant area and follow links that could change the choice. A full business audit should cover every material area. A short question need not end with a limiting-factor diagnosis or a new plan.

## Collision Rules

For the named *70 Profit Tactics* recording or its added ideas on peak-time pricing, feature pruning, useful follow-up and founder strain, read [the tactics map](doctrine/profit-tactics-review.md). It routes all 70 choices without making them mandatory. For the *Money Models* audiobook or print/audio differences, use [the audio edition map](doctrine/money-models-audio-review.md).

When deterministic scores tie, do not pick by wording alone. Validate business context, compare the downstream economic gain and evidence for each candidate constraint, and select the mode that owns the binding step. If current evidence cannot break the tie, ask one short question whose answer would.

- Low conversion plus repeated price objections: diagnose offer, proof, and lead quality before choosing closing.
- More leads requested with poor show or close throughput: sales may bind before demand.
- Pricing requested without delivery cost, conversion, or retention evidence: offer-economics first asks for the math.
- Brand requested for immediate revenue: demand separates long-horizon association from direct-response work.
- Team system requested at an early stage: diagnose whether the owner is importing overhead before the constraint requires it.
- Customer quotes, reviews or screenshots used to persuade buyers: read [proof review](doctrine/proof-review.md). For public review asks, gifts, bonuses or scripts, also read the [channel rules](doctrine/proof-collection.md#keep-it-true-and-worth-the-cost), even for a short rewrite. Customer evidence is not a retention task just because customers supply it.
- Launch pages, signup flows, event copy and partner kits: read [launch review](doctrine/launch-review.md). It links the distinct source pages and scripts; do not blend their offers or treat a screenshot as a tested live flow.
- The named `$100M Journal`, its drawings or draft frameworks: read [Journal review](doctrine/journal-review.md). It separates working notes from finished teaching and links the key visual checks.
- The named `$100M Marketing Roadmap`, marketing maturity or more/better/new channel choices: read [Marketing Roadmap review](doctrine/marketing-roadmap-review.md). Its six marketing stages differ from the ten Scaling Roadmaps. Keep capacity, cash, table conflicts and earlier channel exceptions intact.
- Trust, private disclosures, promises or follow-through: read [Trust review](doctrine/trust-review.md). It separates the risk, past conduct and current incentives, with exact video spans and application limits.
- A named Scaling Roadmap or advice across its stages: use [the ten-source map](doctrine/scaling-roadmaps-review.md). It links the existing stage guides and distinguishes each PDF's main stage, recap and preview, including table/prose conflicts.
- The named `$100M Lost Chapters`, advanced offer choices, hiring limits or maker time: read [Lost Chapters review](doctrine/lost-chapters-review.md). It keeps offer conditions, money examples and team context intact.
- The short `$100M Offers — The Lost Chapter`, Leads bonus chapters, customer selection or lead-filter tradeoffs: read [customer fit review](doctrine/customer-fit-review.md). Keep these sources distinct from the larger collection; it covers the no-customer start and full-path comparison of free, discount and premium offers.
- Content workflow, quality versus volume, useful AI, automation order or founder-brand choices: read [content process review](doctrine/content-process-review.md). It maps the August 12 live, July 19 AI video and August 15 Algorithm video with their own times and limits. Read the supporting source before applying it.
- Bespoke service quotes, weak rep output or repeat jobs: read [service business review](doctrine/service-business-review.md). It links the August 22 event-business case across offer, sales, demand and repeat work, keeping proposals separate from later reports.

- Fast cash requested with neglected customers or broken delivery: customer-value protects trust before promotion.

## Compound Sequences

Modes connect. Targeting can change the offer, the offer can change sales, activation can create proof, and proof can improve demand. Use the table above to find those links without treating every mode as equally urgent.

- Diagnose → Offer & economics → Demand
- Diagnose → Sales → Offer & economics
- Diagnose → Customer value → Offer & economics
- Diagnose → Team & operations

The advisor owns the final synthesis and answer contract.
