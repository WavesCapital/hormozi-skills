# Advisor decision criteria

Use these criteria for substantial diagnosis and linked business choices, not as a required itinerary or response template.

- **Outcome and scope:** Separate the desired result from the proposed tactic. Use the baseline, time horizon and constraints that are actually known.
- **Limiting factor:** When diagnosis is needed, compare volume, conversion, delay, capacity, cost and cash across the relevant path. The smallest percentage is not automatically the main limit. Consider the strongest other explanation that could change the move.
- **Stage fit:** Prefer work the business can deliver and afford. Do not import later-stage systems merely because a source describes them.
- **Return and risk:** Compare credible downstream gain, effort, feedback speed, irreversible cost, buyer value and commitments. Focus does not prohibit independent useful work.
- **Evidence:** Use the relevant topic and source maps. Separate a general teaching, a particular case and an application to this business. Preserve conditions and late corrections. Source search is useful only when it can settle a material question.
- **Money:** Compare like groups and time spans. Distinguish revenue, cash collected, gross profit and net profit; label a remainder by the costs removed. Use complete figures when supplied. Do not double-count costs, treat prepayment as pure profit, or turn arithmetic into a demand forecast.
- **Next move:** Give the answer when settled. Ask or show conditional choices when a missing fact matters. Recommend an experiment when uncertainty needs measurement; justify its size from capacity, risk or the learning needed. A source's round number is not a mandatory quota.

For lead methods and shared-platform risk, use [Demand](doctrine/demand.md). For proof collection, use [Proof](doctrine/proof-review.md). Other routes are in [the topic index](mode-index.md). Choose additional guides only when they can change the decision.

On a follow-up, use new evidence to continue, change or stop. Reuse unchanged context. Do not repeat onboarding, revalidate every link, or defend the prior answer after its assumptions change.
