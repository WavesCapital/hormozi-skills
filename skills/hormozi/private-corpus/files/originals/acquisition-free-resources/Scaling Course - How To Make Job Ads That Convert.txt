How To Make Job Ads That CONVERT




Summary
When advertising to cold audiences AKA low trust audiences we have to put more effort into the advertisement itself.
So even with a good title/pay/EVP, a smaller percentage of people will respond. 
We get over this hurdle by putting the offer in front of more people AKA volume negates luck
If we are not seeing an ROI most of the time, it’s because..
1. The right people never saw it
2. The right people saw it but we’re not compelled to apply
So to make a search fruitful, the right people have to see it. 
When beginning a search you need to determine:
1. Which platform(s) contain the ideal audience?
2. How do I title my job in a way that targets that audience?
3. How do I craft my ad (JD) in a way that compels them to apply?
Example: Customer Experience Associate
1. Which platform(s) contain the ideal audience?
   1. Linkedin: Primary audience here makes above 100k so I would probably not assume they come here
   2. Indeed: Primary audience makes less than 100k so I would post here
   3. Outbound: Sure but would not start here
   4. Social Media: YES, tons of people in Alex and Leila’s network with these skills 
2. How do I title my job in a way that targets that audience?
   1. Why do we choose Customer Experience Associate?
      1. Service = attracts lower skilled worker, lots of transactional service work with low ticket items (desk support, cheap items)
         1. Transactional rather than relational experience
      2. Experience = attracts worker with higher skillset, ability to talk to customers, de-escalate upset customers
         1. Relational experience rather than transactional 
      3. Associate = attracts someone more junior who we can still train up and invest in but who is not SUPER green. If we said “manager” we would likely attract CSMs who are a bit overqualified and would likely grow bored of the role quickly.
3. How do I craft my ad (JD) in a way that compels them to apply?
   1. SEE EXAMPLE FRAMEWORK BELOW


The Framework for a Job Description


HOOK: If you cannot excite someone and speak to your target audience in the first line, the likelihood they continue to read is LOW. Most job reqs lose the best candidates in the first line.


In the first line you want to speak to their dream job, as well as the ways in which we see this role differently. “Why should they apply here and not the other CEX job???”




Example Hook: In this role you are truly the “face” of the company to all customers. When a customer thinks about Acquisition.com, they will associate the company primarily with the experience you provide them with. 




RETAIN WITH VALUES: Now that you have them reading, explain why the values of the company are important, what they are, and how they translate into this role 


Example Retain: That is why it is imperative the person in this role embodies our values - Sincere Candor, Competitive Greatness & Unimpeachable character. As a Customer Experience Associate, you represent the company to all Acquisition.com Scaling Workshop attendees and book customers - you have the opportunity to leave each person better than you found them.




THE MEAT: Now let them know what they will DO, and speak against any fears they may have of this being another pit of drudgery like past jobs.


The Meat Example: You will provide white-glove service by working with each customer to ensure they receive timely, comprehensive responses to inquiries and to improve upon any areas of potential dissatisfaction. This is a relational role, not transactional - we will empower and give you the tools and training to turn every customer experience into a positive one!


You will be expected to provide support to customers with the goal of answering all questions about upcoming workshops and the business’s products in a way that excites customers. To be a successful Customer Experience Associate, one must excel at working directly with clients to problem solve or answer inquiries, think critically, communicate kindly with clients and teammates, and identify opportunities for improved customer experience so you can create lasting relationships.


You are the Acquistion.com Scaling Workshop client’s main POC throughout their workshop customer journey, and therefore you have the opportunity to maintain an enthusiastic “can do” attitude that provides them the necessary resources before, during, and after the event experience!
