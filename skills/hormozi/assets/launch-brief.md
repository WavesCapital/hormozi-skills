## Partner Plan

Name the right group and the reward. Add the notice they need. Add the copy and how you will track it. Name who will help and who will pay. Add checks for fraud.

## When to Stop

Pause if the proof fails. Pause if the team cannot do the work. Pause if a claim is not safe.
