Before using each message, check what the reader has done. Did they sign up, buy or pass? Give them the next step for that state. Do not infer their state from a file name.

## Partner Plan

Why does sharing help the partner's audience? Name that benefit as well as the partner's reward.

Name the right group and the reward. Add the notice they need. Add the copy and how you will track it. Name who will help and who will pay. Add checks for fraud.

For each reward, name who earns it and who gets it. State what counts. Is it a promised reward or a chance to win? Use real terms and counts, not old sample claims.

Give partners the right signup link. Give people who signed up the right join link. Match each reminder to the real date and the step the reader has reached.

## When to Stop

Pause if the proof fails. Pause if the team cannot do the work. Pause if a claim is not safe.
