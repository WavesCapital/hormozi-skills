## Priority

1. Get proof that fits the most important open claim.
2. Use recent and specific proof from the same kind of buyer.
3. Show an honest amount of proof when no one example is strong.

Never call a rare result normal. Do not edit proof so much that it stops being true.
Write the claim the facts support. Five uses of one review still count as one customer result.
