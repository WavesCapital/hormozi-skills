## Checks

- The first offer turns new people into buyers and keeps 30-day profit safe.
- The add-on solves the next problem when the buyer needs it.
- The smaller offer may change the price. It may change how they pay or what they get. It does not hide terms.
- The ongoing offer gives real value each time the buyer pays.
- The full plan fits the team. It keeps trust safe. It keeps claims and consent safe.
