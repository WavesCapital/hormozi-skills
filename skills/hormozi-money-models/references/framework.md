        # Hormozi Money Models Framework

        ## Job
        Map and improve money models using attraction, upsell, downsell, and continuity architecture.

        ## Use Cases
        - How do we get customers to spend more money faster?
- Redesign our monetization stack
- What back-end offers should follow this front end?

        ## Outputs
        - money-model map
- offer-sequence architecture
- monetization economics memo
- ascension ladder

        ## Hard Boundaries
        - Not one-off pricing optimization
- Not launch orchestration
- Not churn-only fixes without a broader model redesign

        ## Composition
        - Composes with: hormozi-pricing, hormozi-customer-value, hormozi-offers
        - Preferred sequence: Use when the bottleneck is the whole monetization system, not a single price or offer tweak.
        - Fallbacks: none
