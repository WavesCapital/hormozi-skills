        # Hormozi Scaling Roadmap Framework

        ## Job
        Diagnose business stage, bottleneck, priorities, and anti-priorities.

        ## Use Cases
        - What stage is this business in?
- What should we stop doing right now?
- What is our next bottleneck?

        ## Outputs
        - stage diagnosis
- evidence memo
- top-3 priorities
- anti-priority and graduation checklist

        ## Hard Boundaries
        - Not detailed team-operating-system design
- Not monetization/ad execution
- Not generic consulting without a stage call

        ## Composition
        - Composes with: hormozi-team-scaling, hormozi-money-models, hormozi-leads
        - Preferred sequence: Use first when the real question is sequencing or stage-fit.
        - Fallbacks: none
