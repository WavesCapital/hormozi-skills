        # Hormozi Lead Nurture Framework

        ## Job
        Improve response, scheduling, follow-up, and show-rate systems.

        ## Use Cases
        - Booked leads are ghosting us
- Improve our show rate
- Write a 7-touch follow-up

        ## Outputs
        - nurture audit
- follow-up sequence
- response-time SLA
- show-rate rescue plan

        ## Hard Boundaries
        - Not top-of-funnel lead generation
- Not live-call objection handling
- Not full launch strategy

        ## Composition
        - Composes with: hormozi-leads, hormozi-closing, hormozi-launch-campaigns
        - Preferred sequence: Use after the lead is captured and before the close.
        - Fallbacks: none
